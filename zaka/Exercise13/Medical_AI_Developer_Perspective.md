# AI Medical Diagnostic Tool
## Developer/Company Perspective (2-3 min talk)

---

## QUESTION 1: What is it?
**An AI system that analyzes radiology images (e.g., X-rays) to detect early signs of diseases like lung cancer.**

### Key Points:
- Deep learning system that analyzes medical images to identify potential disease markers
- Processes X-rays, CT scans, and other radiological images with high precision
- Highlights areas of concern for radiologists to review with confidence scores
- Integrates seamlessly with existing hospital imaging systems (PACS)
- Uses computer vision to detect patterns too subtle for human perception

---

## QUESTION 2: Why is it used?
**To support doctors with faster and potentially more accurate diagnostics, especially in resource-constrained settings.**

### Key Points:
- Acts as a "second pair of eyes" to help doctors avoid missing critical findings
- Reduces diagnostic time from hours to minutes, enabling faster treatment
- Particularly valuable in areas with radiologist shortages or high caseloads
- Improves diagnostic consistency, reducing variability between different doctors
- Can operate 24/7, eliminating backlogs and delays in critical diagnoses
- Not replacing doctors but augmenting their capabilities and efficiency

---

## QUESTION 3: Training data
**Labeled medical imaging datasets (e.g., CT scans, X-rays) with diagnosis information from previous patient cases.**

### Key Points:
- Trained on diverse, anonymized datasets from multiple hospitals and populations
- All training data properly de-identified to protect patient privacy
- Continuous retraining with new validated cases to improve accuracy
- Specific focus on gathering data from underrepresented populations
- Rigorous validation against gold-standard diagnoses by expert panels
- Transparent documentation of dataset characteristics and limitations

---

## QUESTION 4: EU AI Act Considerations

### Key Points:
- Classified as high-risk AI system under EU AI Act (healthcare application)
- Implements rigorous validation protocols exceeding regulatory requirements
- Complete technical documentation and risk management system in place
- Human oversight ensured - system only provides recommendations, final diagnosis by doctors
- Regular conformity assessments and third-party auditing
- Clear explanation of system limitations provided to all medical users
